Save the pages and documents of a site you have to sign in to, using your own account.

This guide is for sites where you have your own, authorized account: a member site, a supplier portal, a course you are enrolled in. Grab All Files runs inside the browser where you are already signed in, so it sees what you see. It does not sign in for you, and it cannot open anything your account cannot open.

The one condition: your own account

Use it only on accounts you are allowed to use, for material you are allowed to keep. Check the site's terms if you are not sure that saving a copy is permitted; a sign-in does not by itself grant that right.

Grab All Files uses the sign-in your browser already has. It does not collect or send your password, the value of a cookie, or anything typed into a form field. It does not automate signing in, get around a sign-in, or solve a CAPTCHA. If the site shows a CAPTCHA, you solve it yourself in the page.

Steps

  1. 1Sign in to the site in the browser where Grab All Files is installed (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Keep that tab open.
  2. 2To save documents: open the page that lists them, open Grab All Files, and use “Bulk File Download”. The files it finds are shown in a list. Filter by type, size or date, tick the ones you want, and save.
  3. 3To save the pages themselves: switch to “Combine pages into HTML”, choose how to look for pages (this page's links, the same path, a sitemap or pasted URLs), check the candidate list, and save. The pages are saved as your browser displays them, so content shown only after sign-in is included.
  4. 4If the pages link to PDF or Office files, the page collector can save them next to the pages: 10 per run on Free, 100 on Pro.

If the session expires partway through

Many sites sign you out after a period of inactivity or after a fixed time. Both tools stop and tell you, and keep what they have already saved.

Where the work happens

Pages and files are read in your browser and written to your computer. They are not sent to the developer's server.

Works well

  • ✓A supplier portal with price lists and drawings attached to many pages.
  • ✓A members' library or a course site where you want the lesson pages and their handouts together.
  • ✓Pages that are drawn by JavaScript after you sign in; the browser's rendered result is what gets saved.

Does not work

  • –Pages or files your account is not permitted to open.
  • –Signing in automatically, or getting past a CAPTCHA.
  • –Content in encrypted or DRM viewers, or in the browser's built-in PDF viewer.
  • –Streamed video; only video or audio linked as a file is saved.

FAQ

Can Grab All Files download from a site that needs a login?

Yes, if you can open the page in the same browser. It uses your existing sign-in. It does not sign in for you, and it cannot open pages your account is not allowed to see.

Does it see or store my password?

No. Passwords, cookie values and form-field contents are not collected or sent anywhere.

Why did some files fail with a sign-out message?

The site ended your session. Open the site, sign in again, and retry the failed files. Files already saved are not lost.

Does it save the pages as well as the files?

Yes. Bulk File Download saves the files; Combine pages into HTML saves the pages, and can save linked PDF and Office files with them.

Is it all right to save everything I can see?

That depends on the site's terms and on who owns the material. Being signed in does not change that. Save only what you are allowed to keep.
