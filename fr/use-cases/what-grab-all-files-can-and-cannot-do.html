What Grab All Files can and cannot do, with the numbers.

Grab All Files is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. It finds files on web pages and saves pages as HTML. This page lists its limits as they are in version 5.8.54, what Free and Pro change, and the pages and files it cannot save, with the kind of tool to use for those.

What the “100” on Pro is, and what it is not

Pro does not have a limit on how many files you download. Its 100 is the number of linked files saved next to the HTML per run in “Combine pages into HTML”: the PDFs and Office files that your saved pages point to. Free saves 10 of those per run. If more are linked, the result screen has a button for the next batch.

Two other numbers are easy to confuse with it. 100,000 is the page limit of one crawl, and 100 is also the number of “Next” pages a Pro scan follows with the longest “Scroll time” setting. All three are separate settings.

FAQ

How many files can I download at once on the free plan?

10 files per save. The same limit applies to a ZIP, a PDF merge and a CSV made from the selection. Select the next 10 and press the button again to continue. Pro has no cap on the number of files.

Is Pro limited to 100 downloads?

No. The file downloader has no file-count cap on Pro. The 100 is the number of linked files saved next to the HTML in one run of “Combine pages into HTML”. A button on the result screen saves the next batch.

How many pages can it crawl?

Up to 100,000 pages in one crawl, at depth “This page only”, 1 to 5, or unlimited. It also reads sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

How large can a ZIP be?

256 MB. Above 200 MB in total a confirmation appears. It does not split a ZIP into several files, so select fewer files if you hit the limit.

Can it save streamed video?

No. It saves video files that a page links to directly, such as .mp4 or .webm. It does not save HLS or DASH streams, blob: playback or videos on streaming sites.

What should I use for a site with thousands of pages?

A desktop website copier. Grab All Files reads each page through a browser tab, which is why it can save pages behind a sign-in and pages built by JavaScript, and also why it is slow at that scale.
