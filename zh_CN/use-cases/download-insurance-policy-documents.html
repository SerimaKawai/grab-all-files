The documents you usually collect for one product

Policy terms (約款)

The long PDF with the full conditions. Often split into general terms and special provisions, each its own file.

Brochure

The sales leaflet, one or two PDFs per product, often with a separate one for each plan or rider.

Key-information document

The explanation of important matters, sometimes one file per sales channel or per age group.

Contract outline and notices

The summary sheet for the contract and any notice that the insurer posts when terms change.

Revision notices

A list or page of changes with dates, as PDFs or as ordinary web pages.

Application forms and guides

Claim forms, change-of-address forms and procedure guides that are linked from the same product page.

Steps

  1. 1Open the insurer's product page, or the page that lists the documents for one product. If a PDF list needs a search first, run the search and open the result page.
  2. 2Open Grab All Files and use “Bulk File Download”. Scan the page. Keep “This page only” if all the files are on that one page, or choose “Depth 1” if the PDFs are on pages linked from it.
  3. 3In the list, narrow by type to PDF, and check titles and sizes. Remove the ones you do not need, such as forms for other products.
  4. 4Set the file name to the PDF's title (use the button that gets the titles of the selected PDFs), and choose how to sort the folders. Save the selected files.
  5. 5Press the file-information CSV button. Open it in a spreadsheet and add your own columns for edition and revision date.

What this saves you from

  • ✓Clicking each PDF on a page that links twenty of them.
  • ✓Files named doc_001.pdf with no way to tell which product they belong to.
  • ✓Losing track of which page a PDF came from, because the CSV lists its source page.
  • ✓A flat folder with every product mixed together.

What it does not do

  • –It does not judge which edition applies to a contract or whether a product suits a customer.
  • –It does not detect that the insurer posted a new revision. You check the notice page yourself.
  • –It does not compare the text inside two PDFs.
  • –It does not open pages that need a login you do not have.

Comparing revisions

The simplest way to compare two editions is to keep both PDFs in the same product folder with the edition or date in the name, then open them side by side. The “Modified” column in the file-information CSV is the date the server reports for the file. It is not the revision date the insurer prints, so do not use it as one.

If the insurer publishes its revision notices as an ordinary web page, you can save that page with “Combine pages into HTML” each time and ask for the change report. The report compares the pages you collected earlier and now by URL, title and body text. It compares web pages, not the inside of PDFs.

常见问题

Can I download policy terms and brochures from many product pages at once?

You scan one page at a time, or follow links from a start page with “Depth 1” to “Depth 5”. The list shows every PDF found, and you choose which to save. On Free, one save is limited to 10 selected files; Pro has no per-save limit.

Does it tell me which edition of the terms a PDF is?

No. The file-information CSV has the file name, title, size, the date the server reports for the file and the page it was found on. The edition and the revision date are things you read from the document and write down.

Can it notice when an insurer revises its terms?

Not by itself. You open the notice page or the product page again and save what changed. For ordinary web pages, the change report compares your latest collection with the previous one of the same site.

Will it name the files by product?

It can use the PDF's title as the file name, and sort folders by type or by site. A product name that is not in the title or the site name is something you add yourself, by renaming afterwards or in your sheet.

Is anything about the policies sent to your servers?

No. Files go from the insurer's site to your computer, and the work happens in your browser. Only the license check, and optional usage statistics if you turn them on, reach the developer.
