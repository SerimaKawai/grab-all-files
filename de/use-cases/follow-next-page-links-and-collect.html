How the page collector finds the next pages

Choose “Follow the links on this page” as the way to find pages. Next-page links are picked up along with the other links, and you review the candidate list before anything is saved.

Collecting files from every page (Pro)

  1. 1Open the first page of the list and open Grab All Files in “Bulk File Download”.
  2. 2Turn on “Scroll to the end and load”, then press “Scan all pages”.
  3. 3Pick “Scroll time” for how far to go: Normal (45 seconds) reads up to 6 pages, Long (90 seconds) up to 20, Thorough (180 seconds) up to 100. If you pick none, it uses Thorough.
  4. 4When it reaches 100 pages and the list continues, press “Read the next 100 pages”.
  5. 5Filter the combined list by type, then save.

On Free, scroll to the end of a long list yourself before scanning, and open each page of the list in turn.

Collecting pages from every page

  1. 1Open the first page of the list, then open “Combine pages into HTML”.
  2. 2Choose “Follow the links on this page” and let it find candidates. “Next” pages are included in the candidate list on both Free and Pro.
  3. 3Check the list and remove what you do not need.
  4. 4Pro: select the pages, choose the look and the form of the saved file, and save them in one run. Free: choose one page, save it, and add the next one to the same saved file.

  • –Very large archives. Past a few hundred pages, a desktop website copier is a better fit.

FAQ

Can it click “Next” by itself and go through all the pages?

Yes. For files, Pro does this with “Scan all pages”. For pages, both Free and Pro follow “Next” to build the candidate list, and Pro can save them all in one run.

Does Free follow “Next” when I collect files?

No. On Free, the file scan reads what is on screen. Scroll a long list to its end yourself, then scan each page of the list in turn.

How many pages does “Scan all pages” follow?

6, 20 or 100 pages depending on “Scroll time” (45, 90 or 180 seconds). If you have not chosen one, it follows up to 100. Beyond that, press “Read the next 100 pages”.

How many pages can I save for free?

One page per run. You can add more pages one at a time to the same saved file. Pro saves all selected pages at once.

What if the next page is loaded by scrolling, not a link?

That is a different case. See the guide on infinite-scroll pages.
