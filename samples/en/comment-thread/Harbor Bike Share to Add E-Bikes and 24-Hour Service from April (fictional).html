Harbor Bike Share to Add E-Bikes and 24-Hour Service from April (fictional)

November 10, 2026

Harbor City has announced a major overhaul of its Harbor Bike Share service, starting April 1. The city, the service and the people in this article are all fictional.

The main changes are listed below. Please share your thoughts in the comments at the bottom of the page.

  • 150 electric-assist bikes (placed first in the hilly Hilltop district)
  • 12 new stations (Southport, Hilltop, in front of City Hospital and more)
  • No more overnight shutdown from midnight to 5 a.m.: the service will run 24 hours a day
  • Fare changes: $1.50 → $1.80 per 30 minutes, monthly pass $20 → $22
  • A new monthly student pass ($15)
  • The Central Station stop will move to the north exit for one year from April because of work on the station plaza

The city will accept feedback until the end of February and plans to settle on a final proposal at the March Transport Committee meeting.
