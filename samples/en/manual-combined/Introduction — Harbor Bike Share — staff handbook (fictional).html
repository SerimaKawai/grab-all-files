Introduction

This handbook explains the daily work at a Harbor Bike Share station. Harbor Bike Share is a fictional service made for the Grab All Files guide.

Read the chapters in order on your first week. Each chapter ends with a short checklist.

  1. Opening a station
  2. Handling a repair report
  3. Closing the day

1. Opening a station

Arrive 15 minutes before opening. Check the dock, the payment terminal and the bikes.

Steps
  1. Unlock the station panel with your staff card.
  2. Turn on the payment terminal and wait for the green light.
  3. Check each bike: tyres, brakes, lights.
  4. Mark any bike that fails a check as “Needs repair”.

2. Handling a repair report

Riders report problems from the app or at the station. Every report gets a number.

Steps
  1. Open the report and read the description.
  2. Find the bike by its number on the dock.
  3. If it is unsafe, lock it and mark it “Out of service”.
  4. Write what you did in the report and close it.

3. Closing the day

Close the station after the last rental returns.

Steps
  1. Check that every dock shows a bike or “Empty”.
  2. Count the bikes marked for repair and note the numbers.
  3. Turn off the payment terminal.
  4. Lock the station panel.
Daily report
  • Rentals today
  • Bikes marked for repair
  • Open repair reports
