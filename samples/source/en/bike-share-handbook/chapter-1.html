1. Opening a station

Arrive 15 minutes before opening. Check the dock, the payment terminal and the bikes.

Steps

  1. Unlock the station panel with your staff card.
  2. Turn on the payment terminal and wait for the green light.
  3. Check each bike: tyres, brakes, lights.
  4. Mark any bike that fails a check as “Needs repair”.
