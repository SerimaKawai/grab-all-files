3. Closing the day

Close the station after the last rental returns.

Steps

  1. Check that every dock shows a bike or “Empty”.
  2. Count the bikes marked for repair and note the numbers.
  3. Turn off the payment terminal.
  4. Lock the station panel.

Daily report

  • Rentals today
  • Bikes marked for repair
  • Open repair reports
