FAQ

Can I choose which pages to include?

Yes. Review page titles before collection, remove pages you do not need, and reorder the rest.

Can I read the result offline?

Yes. Embed images for offline reading, or keep online references for a smaller HTML file. Linked documents can also be saved alongside it.

Is page content uploaded to your servers?

No. Page collection and HTML generation run on your device. The Free plan collects up to 10 pages per run, saved as one HTML file; run it again for the rest. Pro removes the per-run limit and continues automatically up to the chosen safety limit.

How is this different from a one-page saver such as SingleFile?

One-page savers preserve the page currently open. Grab All Files can discover related pages, let you review and reorder them, and combine the selected readable content into one HTML file with a shared table of contents.
