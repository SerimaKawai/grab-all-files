Workflow

  1. 1Open the starting page, click the Grab All Files icon and choose “Combine pages into HTML”.
  2. 2Under “Collection scope”, keep “Only the open page”, or choose “Also find the pages it links to” to list related candidates.
  3. 3Review the candidates. Free keeps exactly one selected and saves it per run; with Pro, “Select multiple candidate pages and combine them (Pro)” lets you pick, remove and reorder pages.
  4. 4Choose the image and linked-file options and save. Pro creates one HTML with a shared table of contents and search across all pages.

FAQ

Can I choose which pages to include?

Yes. Free keeps exactly one candidate selected at a time. Pro lets you select multiple pages, remove what you do not need, and reorder the rest.

Can I read the result offline?

Yes. Embed images for offline reading, or keep online references for a smaller HTML file. Linked documents can also be saved alongside it.

Is page content uploaded to your servers?

No. Page discovery, collection, and HTML generation run on your device. Free saves exactly one chosen candidate; Pro combines multiple selected page bodies. Safety, authentication, permission, source-site, and device-protection limits still apply.

How is this different from a one-page saver such as SingleFile?

One-page savers preserve the page currently open. Grab All Files can discover related pages, let you review and reorder them, and combine the selected readable content into one HTML file with a shared table of contents.

What if a page needs me to sign in?

Collection uses your own browser session and does not collect credentials. If a page is marked “Sign-in needed”, press “Open the site to sign in”, sign in in that tab, then press “Resume”. Pages that still need a sign-in are not saved, and the result says how many were left out.

Why is some text missing from the saved page?

Some sites add text only after you scroll or click. Open “If a page is missing text: content and JavaScript settings”, turn on “Scroll down to load content that appears later” or “Press ‘Show more’ to include the continuation”, and collect again. If the wrong part of the page was kept, use “Select on screen” there to pick the main text.
