Workflow

  1. 1Open the permitted source and choose “Combine pages into HTML” in the extension. Select the pages and note the source’s stated revision yourself.
  2. 2Save the collection with a recognisable before label. Later, manually collect the same site and comparable page scope and save a separate after copy.
  3. 3After the later collection, save the previous-run change report. Check added, updated and removed-or-unavailable entries against collection results. “Save change report vs. previous run”
  4. 4Read the two originals side by side or manually give supported before/after files to an external comparison tool or AI. Confirm any claimed change in the source wording.

FAQ

What does the built-in change report compare?

The report uses this extension’s most recent completed collection before the current one on the same site. It summarises page-URL, title and collected-body changes, not line-by-line or official-revision interpretation. For arbitrary saved PDFs or other before/after files, compare their content in an external tool.

Does a removed entry prove the site deleted that page?

No. It may reflect different selection, collection failure or unavailable content. Compare the page scope and results before treating it as a source deletion.

Does the extension watch for updates or recover old versions?

You collect again manually. Use previously saved material or versions published by the source; automatic monitoring and recovery of an unseen older version are not described.

How do I identify the revision and use Free?

Record the source’s revision yourself in titles or notes. AI output source URLs and capture dates help with tracing. Free selects one page; Pro can collect multiple page bodies.
