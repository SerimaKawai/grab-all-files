Use cases

Pick the task you have in mind. Each guide shows the real steps, the labels you will see in the extension, and what the Free plan covers: 10 files per download and one page per collection.

Product catalogs & datasheets

Save catalogs, spec sheets and datasheets for the models you compare, named by model with their source links kept. Free saves 10 files per download.

IR & financial report PDFs

Save an issuer’s earnings releases, presentations and annual reports by fiscal period, with clear titles and source links. Free: 10 files per download.

Tender & application documents

Save a tender or grant call’s notice, specifications, forms and Q&A in their original formats, named by role and checked against the official list.

File links to CSV

Export a reusable inventory of selected files (URL, filename, title, type, size, date and source page) as a CSV for Excel. Free exports 10 rows at a time.

SDS & technical documents

Save the SDS, TDS and technical PDFs for an exact product, with language and revision in each name and the source recorded. Free: 10 files per download.

arXiv PDFs & research files

Scan an arXiv results page, filter PDFs and save selected papers with titles, folders, ZIP or a file-list CSV. See the real extension screen.

Web tables to CSV

Export supported web tables as CSV, check headers and units, then import them into Excel or give selected files to an external AI tool.

Save & compare revisions

Save material before and after an update, review the previous-collection change report and compare wording with saved originals or external tools.

Rename & organize PDF downloads

Rename PDFs with their real titles before you download, then sort files into folders by type or site. Edit any title; Free saves 10 files per download.

Government documents & guidance

Save a government scheme’s guidance page as HTML and its PDF or Word attachments as files, with sources. Free: 1 page per run, 10 files per download.

Web sources as Markdown

Export collected web text as one Markdown file, per-page notes or a full package, with sources kept for research, writing and reuse in notes or AI.

Save manuals & knowledge pages

Save the online manuals and knowledge articles you may use as one readable reference, with chapters, embedded PDFs and sources kept together.

Collect, read & analyze with AI

Collect web information as readable HTML, or prepare source-tracked files for summarizing, comparing and extracting conditions in an external AI tool.

Combine pages into HTML

Combine related web pages into one searchable, printable HTML with a shared table of contents. Free saves one chosen page per run; Pro combines many.

Local HTML files into one

Merge a folder or ZIP of HTML files on your computer into one offline HTML with a table of contents, embedded images and working links. No upload.

Pages with the original design

Save one page, or many combined, with the original layout, fonts and images in a single offline HTML file. Scripts are removed; nothing is uploaded.

Download all PDFs

Download linked PDFs from a page or its folder in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Filter the results, save 10 files per download for free, or merge PDFs locally.

Bulk-download images

Detect visible, lazy-loaded, background, and blob images, then save selected image files in bulk or export their URLs.

Download files from a page

Find PDFs, Office files, images, ZIPs and CSVs on a web page, filter the list and save them in bulk or as one ZIP. Free saves 10 files per download.

Internal portals & LMS

Save the files you may access from intranets, LMS and member portals with your browser session. No credentials collected; files go to your device.

Merge PDFs locally

Merge PDFs found on a web page into one file inside your browser, with no upload. Set the order by dragging, name the result and keep the originals too.

Try Grab All Files on your browser.

Free saves up to 10 files per download in the file downloader, and one chosen page per run when you combine pages into HTML; that page’s linked files are saved 10 at a time with “Save the next 10”. Pro ($19.99 one-time, up to 3 devices) removes the per-download cap and combines multiple pages into one HTML.
