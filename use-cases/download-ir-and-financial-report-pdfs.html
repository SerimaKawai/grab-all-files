Actual extension screen — IR & financial report PDFs

Best for

  • ✓Collecting a single issuer’s annual or quarterly disclosure package.
  • ✓Preparing selected source documents for a manually requested summary or comparison.

Workflow

  1. 1Open the issuer’s official IR archive and choose a fiscal year or quarter. Review releases, presentation material and published Q&A separately.
  2. 2Open Grab All Files, scan the chosen page, and filter the file types you need. Review the source links and select the relevant files.
  3. 3Verify issuer, reporting period, publication date and revisions. Add a period prefix to titles where useful; work in separate period batches to avoid mixing reports.
  4. 4Check or edit titles, choose the save-name and type/domain folder options, then save files or ZIP. Export File Info CSV to keep a URL record.
  5. 5Read the saved sources or manually give supported files to your chosen AI service. Check units, accounting scope and numbers against the original PDFs. The extension does not make investment decisions or guarantee numeric extraction.

FAQ

Are fiscal years and quarters detected automatically?

No. Review the period labels in the issuer’s documents and choose names or batches yourself. Fiscal years need not match calendar years.

Does the CSV contain financial statement values?

No. File-information CSV records file metadata and links. It does not extract financial statement tables or guarantee complete numerical data.

Can it analyze investments or send reports to AI?

It prepares files and links. You choose and send suitable materials to an external AI service manually and verify its output. No investment recommendation or analysis accuracy is promised.

Can I use this for free?

Free saves up to 10 files per run and exactly one chosen related page as HTML. Images on that page have no plan-based count cap; its directly linked files are saved 10 at a time, and the result screen saves the next 10. Pro ($19.99, one-time) removes the file cap and combines multiple selected page bodies into one HTML. With Free, file saving and file-information CSV each allow up to 10 selected files per operation; repeat in batches for more.

What does the file-information CSV contain?

Selected file URLs, filenames, titles, types, detection confidence/evidence and available size, modification date and source page. Unknown metadata can be blank. It is not a table extracted from the document body.
