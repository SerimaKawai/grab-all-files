Workflow

  1. 1Open the post, video, thread or article whose comments you want in your browser.
  2. 2In Grab All Files, choose “Combine pages into HTML”, set the collection scope to “Current page only” and start.
  3. 3The page opens in a collection tab and scrolls to the end on its own. Buttons such as “N replies”, “Show more replies” and “View more comments” are clicked for you, and the screen shows how many posts have been loaded.
  4. 4Every 1,000 posts, it asks whether to keep going or save what it has so far. If you don’t answer within 10 minutes, it saves the comments loaded up to that point.
  5. 5When it finishes, the comments and replies are saved in one HTML file. To analyze them with AI, follow up with “Copy the text”, “Save as one Markdown file” or “Save as a text file (.txt)”.

FAQ

Which sites can I save comments from?

We’ve tested it on live pages for X (formerly Twitter) posts and replies, YouTube video comments, Reddit threads, Bluesky threads and profiles, and Yahoo! News article comments. On other sites, lists that load more as you scroll can sometimes be scrolled to the end and saved as well.

Are hidden replies included?

Yes. The reply buttons are clicked for you on YouTube (“N replies”, “Show more replies”), Reddit (“more replies”, “View more comments”) and Yahoo! News (“N replies”, “More”). With Pro, replies that continue on another page, such as Bluesky’s “Read more replies”, are followed too, even with “Current page only” selected.

Is there a limit on how many comments I can save?

No. Every 1,000 posts, it asks whether to keep going or save what it has so far. If you don’t answer within 10 minutes, it saves the comments loaded up to that point. When scrolling stops bringing in new comments, it treats the page as finished.

Does it work on the Free plan?

Yes. Loading comments to the end and opening replies work the same on Free and Pro, and we’ve confirmed that the Free plan saves YouTube, Reddit and Bluesky comment sections in full. The difference is how many pages you can save at once: Free saves one page at a time, while Pro combines several pages into one HTML file. Where comments are split across several pages, as on Yahoo! News, Free saves only the first page.

Are ads included?

On X, promoted posts (ads) in the timeline and among replies are recognized and left out. The post you opened is kept.

Does it download the video too?

This page covers saving comments and replies as a page. The video file itself is not downloaded.

What about comments that require signing in?

It saves the comments your browser can display. Members-only comments and others hidden behind a sign-in are not included. On YouTube, the saved count can be slightly lower than the comment count shown under the video, because that number also counts replies that signed-out visitors cannot see.

Are the saved comments sent anywhere?

No. The comments are read in your own browser and saved on your computer. You decide which AI service, if any, gets the copied or saved text. Before sharing or publishing other people’s posts, check each site’s terms and respect the rights of the people who wrote them.
