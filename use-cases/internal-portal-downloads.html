FAQ

Does it collect passwords?

No. It uses the browser session you already have and does not collect credentials.

Can it bypass access controls?

No. It only works on pages and files your browser is already authorized to access.

Is it suitable for confidential files?

Files download directly to your device. Review your organization rules before using any download automation.
